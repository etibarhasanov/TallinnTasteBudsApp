# Listing copy — a draft in my voice, to be rewritten in yours

The App Store page is the map's voice, not an app's. The site's own line —
*all the places in this map I have personally been and approved* — is the whole
pitch, and it is better than anything a description could add. Keep the first
person.

Everything below is a starting point. Character limits are Apple's and are
counted here.

---

## Description — 4000 characters max

```
Every place on this map I have been to myself and approved.

There are no scores and no stars. Being on the map is the verdict — if a place
is here, I ate there and I would go back. That is the whole system.

Seventy-odd restaurants, bakeries, cafés and bars across Tallinn, from a
two-Michelin-star tasting menu on the Noblessner waterfront to Soviet Korean
home cooking fifteen minutes out by bus. Each one has a write-up, the dishes
worth ordering, and photos.

— Find it on the map, or search by name, street or dish
— Filter by what you are actually after: coffee, cheap eats, date night,
  laptop-friendly, vegan, hidden gems
— Sort by what is nearest when you are already out
— Save the ones you mean to try
— Walking directions in one tap
— Nine languages: English, Estonian, Russian, Ukrainian, Finnish, Azerbaijani,
  Portuguese, Spanish and Turkish

Some places give readers a discount. Open one and you get a code to show at
the table.

The map works without a signal — everything is kept on your phone after the
first launch — and it updates itself as soon as you open it, so new places
appear without waiting for an app update.

There is also a radio button. You do not have to press it.
```
*(~1180 characters)*
