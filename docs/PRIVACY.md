## Privacy Policy — Tallinn Taste Buds

*Last updated: [DATE]*

Tallinn Taste Buds is a map of places to eat in Tallinn. It has no accounts, no
sign-in and no advertising, and it is built so there is as little to say here
as possible.

### What the app collects about you

Nothing. There is no account to create, and the app sends no information about
you to anyone.

### Your location

If you tap **Show my location**, the app asks iOS for your position and uses it
for two things: centring the map on you, and sorting places by how near they
are. That position stays on your phone. It is not stored, not logged and not
sent to us or to anyone else. If you never tap the button, the app never asks
for it, and you can withdraw permission at any time in iPhone Settings.

### Places you save

The bookmark list is kept on your device and nowhere else. Deleting the app
deletes it.

### What the app downloads

Each time you open it, the app fetches the map's content — the places, the
text, the photos — from `tallinntastebuds.ee`. Like any web request, this
means the server can see your device's IP address and the fact that a request
was made. That is ordinary web server logging and is not tied to any identity.

### Other people's pages

Two things open a web page inside the app:

- **The discount code**, which is a page on `tallinntastebuds.ee`.
- **Instagram and TikTok posts**, when you tap to watch one.

Instagram and TikTok are not ours and have their own privacy policies, which
govern what they do once one of their pages is open. Tapping the website or
Instagram links in About leaves the app entirely and hands you to Safari.

### The radio

The radio button plays a public internet radio stream, the same way a browser
would. The station is run by whoever runs it, and connecting to it shows them
your IP address as any listener's would.

### Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
